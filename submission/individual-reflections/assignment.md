# Individual Reflection — Assignment

Submit individually.

Please write a concise reflection of 400-800 words. The goal is not to write a long report, but to reflect honestly and concretely on your own experience in the course and project.

Please answer the following questions:

1. Initial expectation

What did you expect to learn or experience when you started the course?

2. Main learning

What was your most important learning from the course or project?

3. Role and contribution in the group

How did you fit into the group, and what did you personally contribute to the project?

4. Future learning

Based on this experience, what are you most interested in learning or developing further?
