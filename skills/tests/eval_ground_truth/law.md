## Notes

- **Structural note (why Law is a comparatively *easy* hard-faculty).** Unlike the Humanities, the Juristische Fakultät lists its **Lehrstühle almost directly** on `.../lehrstuehle-und-personen/`, grouped Bürgerliches Recht / Öffentliches Recht / Strafrecht. Discovery only has to descend Faculty → Öffentliches Recht → chairs (two levels). The hardness here is *disciplinary*, not structural: chair titles are dense German public-law formulas ("Staatsrecht, Völkerrecht, Verfassungslehre und Menschenrechte") that a topical query for "constitutional law" or "AI regulation" must map onto — a good test of the profile→query mapping on non-English, non-obvious chair names.
- **Michèle Finck is the standout tech-regulation hit** — her chair is literally *Recht der Künstlichen Intelligenz*, covering AI law and data protection. She is the Law leg of the interdisciplinary AI-ethics persona (see `interdisciplinary.md`).
- **Excluded from this profile's GT (public-law chairs, but off-interest):** Prof. Dr. Michael Droege (Verwaltungsrecht, Religionsverfassungs-/Kirchenrecht, Steuerrecht) and Prof. Dr. Christian Seiler (Staats- und Verwaltungsrecht, Finanz- und Steuerrecht) — both strong **public law** chairs, but tax/church-law focused, which is off this constitutional/international/tech profile. A tax-law or law-and-religion persona would include them; they are not noise, just off-interest here. (Same pattern as `wiso.md`'s two economics rows.)
