## Notes

- **Structural-hardness note (why this faculty is a robustness test).** The Philosophische Fakultät is a *large, three-level* faculty: the backbone page lists **five Fachbereiche** (FB1 Altertums-/Kunstwissenschaften, FB2 Asien-Orient-Wissenschaften, FB3 Geschichtswissenschaft, FB4 Neuphilologie, FB5 Philosophie–Rhetorik–Medien), and chairs live one or two levels below, in a *Seminar* (here the Philosophisches Seminar under FB5) rather than on a single faculty chair-list page. Discovery must drill Faculty → FB5 → Philosophisches Seminar → Professoren to reach these names — unlike Law/Theology, which list chairs almost directly. This is the "must descend the department tree" hardness, not chair scarcity: the Philosophisches Seminar itself is well-populated.
- **Core vs. adjacent for this sample interest.** The five rows above are the strongly-relevant chairs. Sattig and Wong are the direct hits (theoretical philosophy / philosophy of mind & cognitive science). Corcilius and Schlösser cover the *historical* strand of the same topics (ancient and Kantian philosophy of mind/agency) that this profile explicitly includes. Döring covers theory of emotions (adjacent to philosophy of mind, on the practical side).
- **Deliberately excluded (not noise, just off-profile):** Juniorprof. Dr. Balthasar Grabmayr — philosophy of logic and mathematics; relevant to *theoretical* philosophy broadly but not to this profile's mind/metaphysics focus. A logic-focused persona would add him. Dr. Irina Schumski appears under Professoren but as a non-chair lecturer; not counted as a chair.
- **Affiliation-currency caveat (apply the SKILL §4.7 / 2f check on a live run).** Sabine Döring is listed as *Honorarprofessorin*; she served as a State Secretary (BMBF) and is not a regular full-time chair-holder. A live skill run should flag her availability rather than present her as a standard supervisor — this is exactly the kind of affiliation-currency case Task O added a check for.
