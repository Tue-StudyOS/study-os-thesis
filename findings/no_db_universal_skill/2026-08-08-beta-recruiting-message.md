## Screening — who counts as a tester

Accept anyone who (a) is currently or soon looking for a thesis topic, (b) has a Claude
account, and (c) has not seen the tool or this repo before. **Exclude** anyone who has
worked on the project (Max, Valentin) — they cannot produce an uncontaminated pre-statement.

Do not pre-select for enthusiasm. A tester who expects the tool to be useless is a better
test than one who expects it to work.
